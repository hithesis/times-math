The renamed derivative contains sixteen additional integral operators,
constructed from Termes outlines, with seven native OpenType MATH size
variants per operator. No Times New Roman or XITS outlines are included.

The integral curves and optical-size steps are inherited from Termes.
Bars may extend beyond the integral curve's ordinary ink bounds.
